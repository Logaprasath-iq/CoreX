CONFIGURATOR GUIDE

CUSTOM BUILD FAQS

How does the automated Socket & Wattage validation work?
Our configurator checks CPU sockets against motherboard chipsets (e.g., LGA1700 vs AM5) in real-time. It also aggregates the maximum rated TDP and transient power spikes of your CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, and cooling pumps, warning you immediately if the selected power supply provides less than a 30% safety headroom.
Can I request specific cable comb colors or custom liquid coolant?
Yes. Once your order is placed, an assigned hardware architect reaches out via WhatsApp or phone call to confirm aesthetic specifics: coolant dye color (e.g. COREX Deep Burgundy or Crystal Clear), braided sleeve shades, fan RPM fan curves, and BIOS RGB presets.
What if I already own a GPU or SSD and want to send it in?
You can configure the rest of the system and mark the GPU or storage slot as "Own Component". You can then ship your part to our Chennai lab, where we will inspect, clean, and install it into your custom build with full thermal testing.
What burn-in stress tests and warranty are provided on custom PCs?
Every custom rig undergoes a rigorous 72-hour thermal stress chamber run (Cinebench R23, FurMark, MemTest86, and 3DMark). Systems are backed by a 3-Year COREX Care Warranty with 1-Year Pan-India Doorstep Pickup & Return service, plus all original OEM component warranties.
