PRICING & ASSURANCE FAQS

Are there any hidden assembly, testing, or thermal paste charges?
None. Every prebuilt package and custom rig includes cleanroom assembly, premium thermal interface paste, BIOS optimization, Windows 11 configuration, and our 72-hour stress testing at zero extra cost. The price listed is 100% all-inclusive.
What if a component price drops after I place my order?
We offer a 7-day Silicon Price Match Guarantee. If an OEM component in your selected package drops in official MSRP within 7 days of purchase, we credit the difference back to your payment source immediately.
How is transit damage handled during pan-India courier shipping?
Every shipment is backed by 100% Insured High-Value Cargo coverage. Systems are packed using internal Instapak expanding expanding foam (supporting the GPU and heavy CPU cooler), double-walled corrugate, and heavy-duty strapping. If any transit shock occurs, our courier team replaces it with zero out-of-pocket cost to you.
What payment methods and EMI options are accepted?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay), UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking across 50+ Indian banks, and NEFT/RTGS for enterprise orders. We also offer flexible No-Cost EMI options for up to 12 months with leading banks.
