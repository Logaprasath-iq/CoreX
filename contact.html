FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Are all computer components 100% genuine with Indian warranty?
Yes, absolutely. We source exclusively from authorized Indian national distributors (including Rashi Peripherals, Ingram Micro, Supertron, and Savex). Every component carries its official serial number, holographic distributor seal, and a GST tax invoice enabling direct manufacturer RMA support across India.
How are custom gaming PCs packaged for dispatch outside Chennai?
We inject customized InstaPak expanding foam bags directly inside the chassis to support heavy graphics cards and heatsinks during transit. The outer chassis box is then nestled in a heavy-duty double-walled corrugated shipping crate with insurance coverage against transit damage.
Can I customize components that are not listed in the standard configurator?
Yes. If you have custom requests such as custom sleeved cabling colors, specific hardline acrylic tubing configurations, high-density server ECC memory, or multi-GPU workstations, submit an enquiry or contact us directly on WhatsApp for a personalized bill of materials.
What testing benchmarks are performed prior to dispatch?
Every system is loaded with our rigorous 24-hour diagnostic suite: FurMark 4K GPU thermal stress, Cinebench multi-core CPU load, MemTest86 zero-error RAM verification, and NVMe read/write integrity passes. Benchmark validation charts are included with your delivery documentation.
