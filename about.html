CLEANROOM PROTOCOL

4-STAGE PRECISION ASSEMBLY

Every workstation and gaming rig passes through our ISO-certified cleanroom standard before reaching your battlestation.

STAGE 01

ESD Shielding & Air Shower

Components are unpacked under ionized, particle-filtered laminar airflow stations. Technicians operate grounded ESD-shielded wristbands and conductive smocks, preventing silent silicon degradation.

Zero Electrostatic Voltage Trace
STAGE 02

Thermal Interface & Delid

Application of Thermal Grizzly Kryonaut Extreme or Conductonaut liquid metal using micrometer spreaders. Calibrated torque screwdrivers ensure 0.6 Nm uniform IHS contact pressure without PCB flex.

Up to 12°C Lower Delta-T
STAGE 03

Sleeved Cabling & Combing

Every power delivery line is routed using 16AWG MDPC-X sleeved harnesses with CNC-anodized burgundy cable combs. Dual-chamber cable management keeps airflow corridors completely unobstructed.

Artisan 90-Degree Geometry
STAGE 04

72-Hour Burn-In Stress

Continuous thermal saturation through Prime95 Small FFTs, FurMark GPU torture, and 4-pass MemTest86 DDR5 stability runs. A thermal imaging camera verifies cold-plate delta before final dispatch.

Signed Certification Certificate
