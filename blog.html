HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE GLOSSARY

Demystifying silicon engineering, sub-timings, and thermal physics for competitive PC builders.

MEMORY TUNING DDR5

CAS Latency & Sub-Timings (CL30)

While primary latency indicates clock delay before read execution, secondary sub-timings (specifically tREFI refresh intervals and tRFC) govern 1% low frame-time stability in esports titles by over 18%.

Standard: JEDEC / EXPO +18% 1% Low FPS
THERMAL PHYSICS COOLING

Direct-Die Delidding & Liquid Metal

Removing the copper Integrated Heat Spreader (IHS) to seat copper cold-plates directly against the bare silicon die eliminates thermal barrier junctions, dropping peak junction temps by 15°C to 22°C.

Junction Delta: -22°C Sub-Ambient Flow
POWER DELIVERY ATX 3.1

12V-2x6 PCIe High-Power Safety

The updated ATX 3.1 standard recesses sense pins by 1.7mm, preventing power delivery unless the high-current cable is 100% fully seated, eliminating thermal runaway risk at 600W power loads.

Safety: ATX 3.1 600W Direct Rail
SOLID STATE PCIe 5.0

NVMe Controller Thermal Throttling

Gen5 M.2 drives sustain up to 14,000 MB/s read rates but pull up to 14W. Without dedicated multi-fin heatsinks or heatpipes, the Phison controller thermal-throttles to SATA speeds in under 90 seconds.

Read Rate: 14,000 MB/s Heatsink Required
CHASSIS DYNAMICS AIRFLOW

Positive vs Negative Pressure

Tuning intake CFM to exceed exhaust CFM creates positive interior pressure, forcing air out of unfiltered panel cracks and guaranteeing long-term dust exclusion across high-end glass enclosures.

Tuning: +15% Intake Zero Dust Ingress
SILICON LOTTERY UNDERVOLTING

V/F Curve Optimization

Factory silicon ships with conservative voltage margins. Negative curve undervolting (-25mV to -40mV) allows CPUs and GPUs to sustain higher boost clocks within identical power envelope thresholds.

Offset: -35mV Curve +200MHz Boost Lock
